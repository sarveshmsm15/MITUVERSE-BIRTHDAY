# ✨ MITU — The World's Littlest Universe

A luxury, interactive 3D birthday experience created for **MITRA** (*Mitu Chello, Ammu, Thango*) celebrating **29 November**.

Designed with the aesthetic language of a high-end editorial boutique: cream and blush palettes, oversized serif typography, circular 3D hero compositions, interactive 3D sculpted bunny cake, Thuglish quotes, 29 interactive gift boxes, "Open When" letters, interactive unboxing surprise, and sister photo gallery.

---

## 🎂 Features Included

1. **Top Boutique Navigation & Music Controller**
   - Brand logo `MITU.`
   - Integrated **Remo Birthday BGM (Anirudh Ravichander)** player directly in the top bar with animated equalizer waves and 1-click mute/unmute.
   - Smooth navigation: *Home, Her Story, Little Memories, Love Notes, 29 Surprises, Open When, Surprise*.

2. **Hero Section — 3D Sculpted Bunny Cake & Circular Typography**
   - Custom 3D sculpted bunny cake inspired by Mitra's reference:
     - Adorable light blue-gray fluffy bunny with chubby cheeks, big expressive eyes, rosy cheeks, and cheerful smile.
     - Upright floppy ears with soft pink interior and sugar blossom flower.
     - Paws holding a sugar basket with pastel macarons/eggs and a pink satin bow.
     - Fluffy feet with pink toe beans.
     - **Birthday candle sitting on top of the bunny's head** with realistic flickering flame.
     - Interactive candle blow-out / relight feature with confetti.
   - Curved circular rotating editorial typography: `"THE WORLD IS MORE BEAUTIFUL WITH YOU • MITU CHELLO • AMMU • THANGO • 29 NOVEMBER"`.

3. **Birthday Countdown — 29 NOVEMBER**
   - Editorial countdown clock tracking Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds to upcoming November 29.
   - Built-in celebration mode for the big day.

4. **The Love Letter Section**
   - *"Some people make life beautiful. You're one of them."*
   - Heartfelt, genuine brother-to-sister letter incorporating her nicknames naturally (*Mitu Chello, Ammu, Thango*).
   - Tactile luxury parchment presentation with wax seal 'M'.

5. **Thuglish Quotes — Konjam Love... Neraya Sentiment**
   - 14+ emotional, playful, and touching brother-to-sister quotes in Tamil & English.
   - Animated quote player with category pills, copy button, audio chime, and autoplay toggle.

6. **Ammu's Mood Generator (Daily Sibling Vibe Check)**
   - Interactive mood selector: *Dramatic Queen, Hungry & Dangerous, Boss Girl, Ready To Fight Brother, Sweetest Sister Ever*.
   - Features authentic Tamil sister quotes matched with hilarious and affectionate brother reactions.

7. **Her Special World — Memories**
   - Clean, elegant uniform portrait cards (`aspect-[4/5]`) matching the "Pure Joy" style.
   - Proportioned, luxury modal view on desktop and mobile.
   - Automatically displays your actual photos from `public/assets/memories/memory-1.jpg` to `memory-6.jpg`.

8. **10 Reasons Why You're Irreplaceable**
   - 3-per-row grid of equal cards detailing her smile, attitude, kindness, unique personality, contagious laughter, crazy conversations, adorable expressions, dreams, comforting presence, and simply being herself.

9. **Interactive Birthday Surprise**
   - Realistic luxury blush and gold satin gift box with golden monogram seal.
   - Smooth animated opening with ribbon untying, confetti bursts, celebration cake, and brother's message.

10. **29 Little Surprises (Directly Below Exclusive Surprise)**
    - 29 interactive 3D gift boxes numbered 1 to 29.
    - Each unboxes with sound and confetti to reveal an exclusive message, joke, promise, or memory.
    - Live unboxing progress tracker (*"X of 29 Gifts Unwrapped"*).

11. **Open When You Need Me — Letter Experience**
    - Six interactive wax-sealed envelopes:
      1. *Open when you are sad*
      2. *Open when you are angry with me*
      3. *Open when you miss me*
      4. *Open when you feel alone*
      5. *Open when you need a laugh*
      6. *Open when you forget how special you are*
    - Each opens a heartfelt handwritten Tamil letter from her brother.

12. **Official Royal Citation of Sisterhood**
    - Gold-framed royal certificate honoring Mitra with wax seal and print/save citation feature.

13. **Final Birthday Message & Sister's Blessing Jar**
    - Cinematic closing dedication: *"TO MY FOREVER LITTLE STAR"*.
    - Brother's sign-off: `"With endless love, Your Brother ❤️"`.
    - Interactive Blessing Jar to drop birthday wishes that persist in `localStorage`.

14. **Sparkle Cursor & Web Audio Sound Synthesizer**
    - Delicate trailing gold/rose star dust cursor on desktop.
    - Dual audio engine: plays `/assets/music/remo-birthday.mp3` with automatic ambient synthesizer fallback.
